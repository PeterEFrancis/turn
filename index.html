A pattern starts with a turn.

1. Set up your cards

Choose 3–8 perimeter holes and the number of tablets. Each pick turns a tablet by one hole: 360° divided by its hole count.

2. Thread your palette

Select a color and click or drag across the threading chart. Select ∅ to leave a hole empty. Rows A, B, C… are the holes; each column is one tablet. Click S or Z to change its threading direction.

3. Plan the weave

F turns the top of the card away from you. B turns it toward you. Click individual turning cells, or apply a repeating sequence to the whole band.

Reading the preview

The preview is a schematic of warp colors and stitch direction, viewed from above. Hole labels are read from the same card face. Look at the labelled card face from your right, with the selected starting hole upper-near and the next letter upper-far. Labels run clockwise. The first forward turn brings the starting upper-near hole to the surface, then works backward through the labels. On fully threaded cards, reversing repeats the previous thread. Empty-hole drafts show the occupied thread above the weft; uninterrupted floats appear as longer stitches.

Different hole counts produce different sheds and floats. This draft does not simulate tension, tablet thickness, or the exact structure of long floats; sample your design before a full warp.

Start from a pattern

Browse the pattern library for vines, braids, scrolls, and diamonds. The detailed bands load their own four-hole threading and individual turning plans. Everyday drafts use your current card type and dimensions. Applying a pattern replaces your current draft; Undo restores it.

Keep your work

Save draft downloads an editable JSON file. Use Open draft to continue it later. Export a complete SVG chart or print your threading and turning instructions.
